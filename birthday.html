Happy Birthday!
Our Proprietress

Today is not just a birthday, it is a celebration of grace, leadership and selfless sacrifice.

On behalf of all the staff, students and parents of Flostev Schools, we join the world to celebrate an extraordinary woman - a mother to many, a leader with vision, and a proprietress with a heart of gold. Your journey is an inspiration to all of us.

From the very first day you laid the foundation of Flostev, you did not just build classrooms and walls, you built destinies. You built hope for every child that walks through our gates. You taught us that education is not just about books, but about character, discipline, love and fear of God. Through your wisdom and motherly guidance, Flostev has grown from a small dream to a great citadel of learning we are all proud of today.
On this special day, our prayer for you is simple but deep: May God Almighty bless you abundantly. May He grant you long life in good health, overflowing joy, peace that passes all understanding, and more grace to lead Flostev to greater heights. May your children and grandchildren rise and call you blessed. May everything you lay your hands upon prosper.

We love you, we appreciate you, and we are grateful to God for your life, Ma. Thank you for being the light of Flostev Schools.

We Love & Celebrate You, Ma! 🎂
