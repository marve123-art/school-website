Academic Programmes

Building qualitative future leaders through a strong and balanced curriculum

Early Years

Creche (3 months – 2 years)

A warm, stimulating environment focused on care, play-based learning, and early social development.

Nursery (2 – 5 years)

Foundation stage that introduces literacy, numeracy, creative arts, and basic life skills through engaging, child-centred activities.

Primary School

Primary 1 – 6

A solid foundation in core subjects: English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Social Studies, Civic Education, and Computer Studies. Strong emphasis on reading culture, handwriting, and character formation.

Junior Secondary

JSS 1 – 3

Broad-based curriculum covering English, Mathematics, Basic Science & Technology, Social Studies, Business Studies, French, Computer Studies, and Creative Arts. Students are prepared for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

Senior Secondary

SSS 1 – 3

Students choose from Science, Arts, or Commercial streams according to their strengths and future career goals. Thorough preparation for WAEC (SSCE), NECO, and JAMB examinations.

Admission Process

Simple steps to enrol your child at Flostev Group of Schools

1

Visit or Contact Us

Visit any of our campuses or contact us via phone/WhatsApp to express your interest and get more information.

2

Obtain Admission Form

Collect the admission form from the school office or request it online. Fill it carefully with accurate details.

3

Submit Required Documents

Submit the completed form with the following documents:

  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Previous school report card (if transferring)
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Medical report (if available)
4

Entrance Assessment

Your child will take a simple entrance assessment (and interview where necessary) to determine the appropriate class placement.

5

Offer of Admission

Successful candidates will receive an official offer of admission letter from the school.

6

Payment & Enrolment

Pay the required fees to secure the place. Once payment is confirmed, your child is officially enrolled.

Note: Admission is subject to availability of space. Early application is strongly advised as spaces are limited.
